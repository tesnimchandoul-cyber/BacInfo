# 🎓 BacInfo

> A comprehensive digital educational platform specifically designed to organize and share resources, summaries, exercises, and videos for Computer Science students in the Tunisian Baccalaureate.

---

## 🌟 Overview
**BacInfo** is built to provide an all-in-one hub for Tunisian IT Baccalaureate students. It centralizes learning materials across various core subjects and languages, making exam preparation structured, smooth, and highly accessible.

## 📚 Subject Pages & Structure
The platform includes dedicated pages for every subject and section:

* 💻 `algorithmes.html` - Algorithms & Programming
* 📐 `math.html` - Mathematics
* ⚡ `physique.html` - Physics
* ⚙️ `sti.html` - Sciences and Technologies (STI)
* 🧠 `philosophie.html` - Philosophy
* GB `anglais.html` - English
* FR `français.html` - French
* ES `espagnol.html` - Spanish
* AR `arabe.html` - Arabic
* 🎥 `videos.html` - Educational video resources
* 📞 `contact.html` - Contact and support page

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **HTML5 / CSS3:** For responsive design, custom layouts (`matiere.css`, `style.css`, etc.), and modern UI.
* **JavaScript (`main.js`):** For dynamic interactivity and user experience.
* **GitHub Pages:** For fast and free live deployment.
